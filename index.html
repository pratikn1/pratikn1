Bidirectional Twisted-String Actuator

A differential tendon-drive actuator, solved analytically for zero-slack take-up before any parts were cut.

  • Derived the take-up relation R(θ) = dΔL/dθ governing the differential twisted-string drive
  • Held 1:1 spur gears and concentric pulleys to <0.05 mm to eliminate backlash across a full 180° range
  • 0.26 N·m continuous bidirectional torque — 46% smaller package, 6% better torque-to-weight than the prior design

Personal Project

Robot-Joint Actuator

A CAD design study integrating a frameless torque motor, a strain-wave (harmonic drive) reducer, and an off-axis absolute encoder into a single robot-arm joint.

  • Designed the input shaft as the load-bearing interface, stepping between the motor rotor bond diameter and the reducer's wave-generator bore (H7 fit)
  • Selected and sized an output bearing to carry the joint's radial, axial, and moment loads — the one interface not defined by either COTS datasheet
  • Full stack-up: custom housings, output flange, encoder readhead bracket, seals and service routing
  • Deliverables: isometric view, cross-section view, and STEP export

Registered Color Image Synthesis from SFDI Data

A Python imaging pipeline that reconstructs clinically-readable color images directly from multispectral SFDI reflectance maps, improving visualization for burn-wound classification.

  • Weighted SFDI reflectance maps (471/526/659 nm) by measured LED spectra and CIE 1931 color-matching functions to compute pixel-wise XYZ, then converted to sRGB
  • Synthesized RGB images preserved burn-site geometry and stayed spatially aligned with the source SFDI data
  • Supported a published SPIE conference paper and raised burn-classification accuracy from ~50% to 80%+
